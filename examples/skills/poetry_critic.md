---
name: poetry_critic
description: "Critique a poem for imagery, rhythm, structure and emotional effect, then score it"
---
When asked to critique a poem, follow these steps:

1. Read the whole poem once for its overall emotional effect.
2. Identify the dominant image or metaphor and quote the lines that carry it.
3. Comment on the rhythm, line breaks and sound (alliteration, assonance).
4. Summarize the theme in one sentence.
5. Give one concrete suggestion to strengthen the poem.

Keep the critique under 150 words and end with a 1-10 score.
